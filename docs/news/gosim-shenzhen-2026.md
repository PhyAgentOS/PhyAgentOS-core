# Meet PhyAgentOS at GOSIM Shenzhen 2026

[English](gosim-shenzhen-2026.md) · [简体中文](gosim-shenzhen-2026_zh.md) · [Back to README](../../README.md#news)

**PhyAgentOS has been selected for GOSIM Shenzhen 2026 Spotlight.** We will also present our work in the Open Source Robotics track. Join us in Shenzhen on October 16–17 to meet the team and see PhyAgentOS demos.

## Spotlight × Open Source Robotics

GOSIM Spotlight brings selected open-source projects to an international audience through project presentations and demo booths. PhyAgentOS is included in the [official Spotlight Shenzhen 2026 selection](https://spotlight.gosim.org/shenzhen2026/).

PhyAgentOS is an open-source framework for physical agents with recursive self-improvement. It connects cognitive planning, Physical Execution, and task verification, turning verified experience into reusable Skills and Lessons. Our goal is to bring Agent capabilities into the physical world, across robots and simulators.
